Get Presenter

Install once.
Open your next talk.

Prebuilt releases for Apple silicon macOS and x86_64 Linux. You can also build from source.

Build instructions ↗

  1. Install the desktop app

    Run this command in your terminal.

    curl -fsSL https://corepunch.github.io/presenter/install.sh | sh
    Browse releases ↗

  2. Generate and open your deck

    Copy the agent prompt above, attach your material, and review the generated presentation.

    presenter "My Talk.slides"

  3. Share the audience window

    In your meeting app, share the window showing your slides. Keep Presenter View visible to you for the transcript and navigation. Share the individual window to keep your notes private.
